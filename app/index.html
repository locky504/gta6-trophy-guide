Roadmap to the platinum

Built from the official descriptions. One playthrough is enough, there are no online trophies, and free roam opens after the story — except for the one warning in stage 2.

⚠ The one thing that may not wait — Positive Mental Attitude. One optional meditation at Jason’s house is only available in Chapter 1: after the Down to Earth mission, follow Lucia to the balcony on a following morning and accept when she offers to teach you. It adds to the meditation counter but is not listed as a spot, and it is unconfirmed whether the trophy needs it — do it anyway, and keep a separate save. Every other trophy can be earned after the story.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story

    Chapters 1–4 and the final mission. Enjoy it at your own pace: 12 story trophies unlock as you go, and the 100% checklist appears under Touch Pad > Progress once the story is done. Cheats switch trophies off, so leave them alone for the whole run.

    Just Like Paradise · Paradise Lost · T**s Ass Drug Money · Vice City, Baby! · Top Of The World, Ma · You Gotta Know When to Get Off · Trust · Break the Bank · Family Matters · DWNFALL · Hi-Jacked · Snow Storm

  2. Stage 2 — Do the Chapter 1 meditation

    Before Chapter 1 ends: the balcony meditation with Lucia (see the warning above). Then meditate at all 18 spots across Leonida — a spot only counts once its closing zen animation has played.

    Positive Mental Attitude

  3. Stage 3 — Stranger strands

    Finish the mission chains for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, plus three more stranger strands, and hunt Bret’s unique animal. These feed the silver Strangers & Freaks trophy.

    Road to Rehabilitation · This Was No Boat Accident · The Lone Rider · Supply & Demand · Spin On This! · Strangers & Freaks · Fair Game

  4. Stage 4 — Activities, crime & the odd ones

    Stunts, shooting ranges, drone races, races, fishing, arcades, challenges, partner robberies, vaults and the hidden one-offs. Work through the checklist below; the vague ones are flagged where no guide has confirmed a method yet.

    Cunning Stunts · Master of None · The Shootist · Top Fun · Earn Cash, Not Kudos · Baited My Tackle · Stick Fiddler · One Last Thing · Hot Together · Retail Therapy · Gunslinger · Catch Me If You Can · Challenging Tank · Sneakthief · Maximum Withdrawal · A Classic Blitz Play · Pest Control · In The Beginning… · Capital Offense · Let’s Go Bowling · Personal Best · Actions Speak Louder · Bad Luck, I Guess · The Truth Is Out There · Ten Pin

  5. Stage 5 — Collectibles, money & 100%

    Cards, WAiNK scans, vehicles, exploring Leonida, laundering, shopping and garages. Then finish the 100% checklist for the gold A Long Road — and the platinum pops with it.

    Don’t Touch My Deck · You WAiNKer! · Whip It Good · The Goodtime State · Dime on the Dollar · Treat Yo Self · Little Boxes · On the Spot · A Long Road · Vice Got a Price!

Sources & how this guide was built

Trophy names, descriptions, grades and hidden flags are the official PlayStation 5 list as Exophase and PSNProfiles published it on 2026-11-18 (the two agree on every name, description and grade), cross-checked on 2026-11-21 against Push Square, Planet GTA 6, GTABase, Insider Gaming and EGamers. Every guide above is written in this entry’s own words; each one names the source its facts come from, with a link. Where no published guide has confirmed a method yet, the trophy says so instead of guessing — five trophies are flagged unconfirmed.

Notes on the data

  • Grades: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze. Push Square’s guide counts 5 silver and 47 bronze instead — it lists Maximum Withdrawal as bronze; this guide follows Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree on silver.
  • No online trophies: all 55 are single-player and offline. Choosing “Online” in the kind filter shows that on purpose.
  • Hidden trophies: 28 of the 55 stay hidden on PlayStation until earned. The spoiler setting hides their names, descriptions and guides until you tick them off.
  • Counts the game tracks: 250 of 397 stunts, 42 shooting-range challenges, 14 drone-race locations, 18 meditation spots, 18 card sets, 80 challenges, 500 profile actions.
  • Time: PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 at about 200 hours; Push Square says 300 hours or more. The game released 19 November 2026, so early guides are still being confirmed — check the linked sources for updates.
